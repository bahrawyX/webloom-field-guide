WEBLOOM — SOURCE ATTRIBUTION AND PUBLICATION NOTES

Webloom is a distinct identity study built from a frozen offline replica of
softglossary.space, a 2022 HAW Hamburg master's thesis project by Gina Bartzok
and Isabell Henninger. This attribution records provenance and does not imply
affiliation, endorsement, ownership transfer, or a license grant.

The Inspiration collection was frozen from the public Are.na channel
“.softInspiration” (channel 2063847):
https://www.are.na/soft-glossary/softinspiration

Screenshots in that collection remain paired with the external destinations
recorded by the source collection. Their inclusion does not imply that Webloom
owns or is affiliated with those projects.

The local build also retains the source publication's educational glossary
snapshot and bundled educational typefaces. Rights and license status for the
replica, third-party screenshots, glossary content, and fonts have not been
independently verified. Complete that review before treating this preview as a
finished production publication.
